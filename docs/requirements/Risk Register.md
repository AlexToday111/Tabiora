# Risk Register


## 1. Scoring criteria

### Probability (no random numeric percentages)

| Level | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Low | No known trigger in the current design; would require a decision or external event that is not currently pending. |
| Medium | Plausible within the project timeline; depends on an open D-ID, an unconfirmed decision, or an external party whose behaviour is not yet agreed. |
| High | Expected to occur unless a decision, design change, or agreement is made; triggered by one or more currently open D-IDs or known unmitigated dependencies. |

### Impact

| Level | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Low | Local inconvenience; recoverable within minutes; no customer money at stake; no data exposure. |
| Medium | One or more customers or one restaurant affected; recoverable within the same day; limited financial impact; no confirmed cross-boundary data leak. |
| High | Customer-facing double charge or double booking; restaurant unable to serve confirmed guests; cross-restaurant data exposure; extended booking/payment outage; or any event that blocks a Must Have requirement. |

### Residual risk

Residual risk = likelihood × impact **after** the listed mitigation is in place and its detection signal is being observed. Where mitigation depends on an open D-ID, residual risk is stated for “mitigation partially applied” and must be re-scored once that decision is recorded.

## 3. Notes on ownership and detection

- **Owner roles are proposals.** Product Owner, Architecture Owner, Integration Owner, Security Owner, QA/Test Owner, Operations Lead, Project Coordinator/Scrum Master, and Restaurant Manager (staff side) are role labels used for planning. They are **not** production on-call assignments and do not imply staffing commitments.
- **Detection signals must be observable.** “No detection” is not an acceptable residual state for any High-impact risk. Where the detection depends on a parameter that is still TBD (e.g. the undelivered-handoff window under D-06, the reconciliation SLA under D-04), the register states that dependency explicitly rather than inventing a value.
- **Residual risk is stated against partial mitigation.** Because D-01–D-13 are Open, most mitigations are **partially applied**. Residual risk is downgraded only when the corresponding D-ID records a decision and the detection signal is being observed. This keeps the register honest against the current design-stage status.
- **Cross-reference.** Each risk carries the D-IDs, Q-IDs, and FR/NFR/AC references affected. If a decision is recorded in `open-questions.md`, re-score the affected rows and note the change with a date and reviewer.
